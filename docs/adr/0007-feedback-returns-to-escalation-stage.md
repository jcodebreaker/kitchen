# Feedback returns to the stage the job escalated from

When a job goes to needs-human, the engine records the stage it left (`escalatedFrom`), and `kitchen feedback` sends it back to that stage with a fresh round budget, not always to build: a job that escalated from intake, spec or repro would otherwise skip its spec or its reproduction, and one stuck in verify or report would rebuild for nothing. The operator can name an earlier stage with `kitchen feedback <id> --to <stage>`: only intake, spec, repro, build, drives or verify, and only one at or before the stage the job escalated from (build for a job with none). A later target would skip a stage the job never passed (spec when it escalated from intake), and gate, toolkit, review, ready or report would let it reach done without being gated, driven and verified again on the new work. The machine refuses any other target with the reason, and the feedback text is not kept. Jobs from before this change, and `done` jobs, go to build.
